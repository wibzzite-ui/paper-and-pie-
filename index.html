Our Philosophy & Soul

Where Stories Meet Flavors

Paper and Pie is more than just a café – it's a sanctuary for book lovers, coffee enthusiasts, and pie aficionados. With locations across Bangalore's thriving hotspots including Whitefield, Indiranagar, and HSR Layout, we combine the warmth of freshly baked goods with the charm of a reading nook.

Our handcrafted pies, artisanal coffee, and carefully curated library create the perfect ambiance for conversations, creativity, and contemplation.

Work-friendly spaces with conference rooms and podcast/recording facilities make us the ideal spot for professionals, creators, and remote workers seeking a productive yet cozy environment.
